## Mark Manson: The Art of Intentional Values

### Three Principles of Good Values

**1. Controllability**
Focus on things you can control — your own actions and honesty — not the feelings of others.
- You can control: effort, discipline, honesty, integrity, learning
- You cannot control: how others perceive you, their opinions, market outcomes
- 🔑 Focus energy on the controllable, let go of the rest

**2. Reality-Based**
Align values with the truth of the world, not fantasies.
- Comfortable lies are more dangerous than uncomfortable truths
- What you think should happen vs what actually happens — reconcile the gap
- 🔑 Face reality clearly, even when it stings

**3. Socially Constructive**
Values must contribute positively to others, not just yourself.
- Pure self-interest corrodes relationships and reputation
- Win-win over extract-and-exploit
- 🔑 Build something that outlasts you

### The Power of "Not Giving a F*ck"

- NOT about apathy — it's about comfort being different
- Prioritize what truly matters over external validation
- Choose your struggles consciously — every value has a cost
- 🔑 Say "no" to the urgent so you can say "yes" to the important

### Self-Reflection Techniques

**Time Audit:**
Track how you spend your week — does it align with what you claim to value?
- Actions reveal values, not words
- If calendar doesn't match stated priorities, something's wrong

**Memento Mori:**
Reflect on death — it filters out trivial worries and clarifies what matters.
- What would you regret not doing?
- What would you regret doing?
- Use this lens on every major decision

**The Law of Avoidance:**
We sabotage success because change threatens our existing identity.
- Holding identity loosely → remain open to new, better experiences
- Growth requires leaving the old self behind
- 🔑 If it's comfortable, it's probably holding you back

### The Problem with Optionality

- Many choices feels like luxury but leaves people adrift
- Hardship can clarify purpose — necessity leaves no room for aimless wondering
- 🔑 Constraints breed focus; unlimited options breed paralysis
