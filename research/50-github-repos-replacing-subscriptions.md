## Jacob-relevant shortlist
- Docs/knowledge: AppFlowy, Standard Notes, Joplin, Documenso
- Media: Immich, Immich-like self-host, Shotcut, Kdenlive, Darktable, Cap
- Automation/agents: n8n, Activepieces, CoPaw, Open WebUI, LobeChat, LocalAI/Jan
- Self-host infra: Coolify, Nextcloud, Meilisearch, Uptime Kuma, GlitchTip
- Security: Vaultwarden (self-host 1Password)
- Analytics: Plausible, PostHog

## Caveat
Every repo above is a **starting point, not a free replacement**. Most of these are
self-hosted services: you trade a monthly fee for your own hardware, uptime work,
backups, upgrades and (often) a nontrivial setup. Verify stars/activity/maintenance
before migrating anything; the star counts in the thread are from Oct 2026.
