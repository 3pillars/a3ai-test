# Hunter Thesis Counter-Signal Daily Morning Briefing

Run this every morning at 7:30 AM PST via Telegram.

## Instructions

You are monitoring David Hunter's bull thesis (final parabolic melt-up in stocks/metals into mid/late 2026, driven by lower rates + lower oil from Iran resolution) and looking for counter-signals that could disprove or invalidate it.

Run the following checks and compile into a concise morning briefing:

---

### 1. Oil & Geopolitics (Daily)
- Check current Brent and WTI crude prices
- Strait of Hormuz status / Iran conflict developments
- Any ceasefire or de-escalation news
- Flag if: oil >$90 sustained, Gulf supply disruptions >6M bpd, or structural tightness forecasts rising

### 2. Interest Rates & Yields (Daily)
- Current 10-year and 2-year Treasury yields
- Fed statements or Fed speakers today
- Latest CPI/PPI data if released
- Flag if: 10y yield >4.5% or rising, not declining meaningfully

### 3. Market Performance (Daily)
- S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, Russell 2000 levels
- Compare to Hunter targets (S&P ~8,700-9,500, Nasdaq ~27k-32k)
- Flag if: range-bound, no parabolic move, failing to make new highs

### 4. Sentiment (Daily)
- AAII survey, CNN Fear & Greed, VIX, put/call ratios
- Flag if: fear/skepticism persists, no bullish euphoria developing

### 5. Economic Stress Signals (Daily)
- Unemployment claims, ISM manufacturing/services PMI
- Yield curve status, banking/private credit stress
- Flag if: leading indicators worsening

### 6. Geopolitical & Policy Shocks (Daily)
- Iran/Middle East escalation check
- Fed/ECB policy surprises
- Flag if: any shock removing Hunter's expected catalysts

---

## Output Format

Morning briefing should be concise — bullet points, emoji for quick scanning, and a one-line "Hunter Thesis Score" at the end:

```
🌅 Hunter Counter-Signal Briefing — [DATE]

🛢️ Oil: $XX | [trending up/down/neutral] | Flag: YES/NO
📉 10Y Yield: X.XX% | [trending] | Flag: YES/NO
📊 S&P: X,XXX | [above/below/neutral] | Flag: YES/NO
😱 Sentiment: [Fear/Greed/Neutral] | Flag: YES/NO
📉 Economy: [stress/growth/neutral] | Flag: YES/NO
⚡ Shocks: [none/listed] | Flag: YES/NO

🎯 Hunter Score: X/6 signals failing
```

Keep it under 300 words. Focus on what changed overnight. Alert immediately if any threshold is breached (oil >$95 for 2+ days, 10y >4.5%, S&P breaking lower with volume, VIX spiking).

---

## Alert Thresholds (trigger immediate notification)

- Oil >$95 sustained for 2+ days
- 10Y yield >4.5% and rising
- S&P breaks below recent support with high volume
- VIX >25 without clear catalyst
- Any Iran escalation news that could remove rate-cut catalyst
