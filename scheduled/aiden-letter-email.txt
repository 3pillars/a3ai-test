Reminder for you, Jacob:

Aiden's Social Science class (Mrs. Cerda) is doing time capsules. Your job as the parent is to write him a personal letter that he'll seal, unread, and open at the end of the school year.

The rules from the teacher:
  - Write a letter to Aiden about any topic you choose.
  - Do NOT let him read it before it's sealed.
  - Seal it in an envelope.
  - Do NOT write his name on the envelope. Use the SECRET CODE the teacher gave him — write that code on the outside of the envelope AND on the outside of his time capsule.
  - Due on his block day next week: Wed 8/26 (period 6) OR Thu 8/27 (period 7).
  - Teacher: Mrs. Cerda, cerda_eilis@cusdk8.org

Below is a sample letter to get you started. Swap in the details in [brackets], make it yours, then hand it to Aiden sealed.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Dear Aiden,

By the time you open this, a whole school year will have passed — and you'll have grown in ways neither of us can picture right now. I wanted to leave you a few words from your dad at the start of it.

First: I'm proud of you. Not for any grade or trophy, but for the person you are — [curious / kind / stubborn in the best way / the one who makes us laugh at dinner]. Those things matter more than anything a report card can measure.

This year, I hope you'll [try something that scares you a little — a new sport, a hard class, speaking up when it's easier to stay quiet]. You don't have to be the best at it. You just have to give it an honest shot. The trying is where you grow.

A few things I want you to remember, whatever this year throws at you:
  - Hard things are supposed to feel hard. That's not a sign you're failing — it's a sign you're learning.
  - Be a good friend, and choose good friends. It's one of the most important decisions you'll make and nobody talks about it enough.
  - When you mess up — and you will, everyone does — own it, fix what you can, and move on. That's what strong people do.
  - Your mom and I are always in your corner. Always. There is nothing you could do that would change that.

I already can't wait to hear how this year went — what surprised you, what you're proud of, what you'd do differently. Come find me after you read this and tell me everything.

I love you, buddy. Have a great year.

Love,
Dad

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Tip: keep a photo of the sealed envelope + the secret code somewhere, in case you need to reference which capsule is his.
