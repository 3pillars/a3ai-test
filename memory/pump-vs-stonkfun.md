# Pump.fun vs StonkFun — Detailed Comparison (2026-09-18)

## Source: Unknown analyst, shared by Jacob

## Design (StonkFun wins)

- Built around "what does this coin trade against?" not "another SOL curve"
- Quote asset is the product: xStocks (SPYx, NVDAx), PreStocks, ZEC, WBTC, HYPE, currencies, commodities, or another meme — stock × meme loop as first-class, not a toggle
- Holder rewards in the paired asset — getting paid in SPYx/ZEC/etc. is a reason to stay, not just snipe
- ~60% of platform revenue buys and burns STONK; STONK is paired with SPYx, so the platform token demos the thesis
- Extra: locked Raydium liquidity, auto creator payouts, StonkNFT as tradeable LP claim

Pump's Custom Pairs (announced ~Sep 9, weeks after StonkFun and days after StonkFun's fee spike) is a feature on top of the same machine. Same primitive, different incentives.

## Execution / User Capture (Pump wins)

- Pump is still the default Solana launch surface: tens of thousands of launches/day
- ~$120M spot volume, ~$1.65M fees/24h vs StonkFun ~$0.63M fees
- Lifetime Pump fees in the billion-dollar range
- When Pump clones, it puts the feature in front of the existing casino

## For This Trend Specifically

| | Pump.fun | StonkFun |
|---|---|---|
| Trend fit | Fast follower | Native |
| User acquisition | Best on Solana | Narrative spike, smaller base |
| Why a user stays | Next launch, cashback, liquidity | Quote-asset rewards, pair identity |
| Value to token | ~50% custom-pair rev to PUMP buybacks | ~60% platform rev to STONK burn |
| Risk | Meta leaves → meme factory | Meta leaves → pairing pad with less default flow |

## Third Rail: Pons on Robinhood Chain

Pons is currently taking more fees than either on many days — because that's where native stock tokens + mobile retail already sit. Solana stock pairs are still mostly xStocks wrappers. Solana stock pairs are fighting over a meta Pons already lives inside.

## Practical Split

- **Pump:** flow and discovery
- **StonkFun:** when the pair IS the trade (NVDA-denominated meme, ZEC-reward cat, SPYx-priced platform token)
- Design ≠ distribution. StonkFun has the first. Pump still has the second.
