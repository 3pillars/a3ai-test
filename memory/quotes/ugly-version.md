# Ugly Version - Start Before You're Ready

**Source:** Newsletter "my ugly version, episode 1" (2026)

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Start Ugly
> "As you start to walk on the way, the way appears." — Rumi

- You can't plan your way into clarity. You have to act your way into it.
- Anything beautiful in this world started out ugly.
- The only people who create beautiful things are those willing to do the ugly version first.
- You won't see the beautiful version yet. Just supply enough faith to get started.

### 2. Violent Consistency → Quality
> "The works of highest quality were all produced by the group being graded for quantity."
> — Art & Fear (ceramics class experiment)

- Quantity is a necessary precursor to quality.
- You cannot create once and hope for it to be perfect. You have to create a lot. Every single day.
- Quality is a byproduct of quantity.
- 5 years, 2 sends/week, never missed an issue — violently consistent, quality followed.

### 3. Think in Decades, Act in Days
> "How would you approach what you're doing right now if you knew you'd be doing it for the next ten years?"

- Daily discipline without long-term direction is dangerous.
- Prevents: chasing trends, value extraction over creation, monetization over energy/passion.
- Apply to: relationships (not transactions), training (not injury), work (not arbitrary quarterly results).

### 4. Trust is the Atomic Variable
- Everything built on top of trust.
- 50 years ago: trust centralized (P&G, Sears dominated airwaves)
- Now: trust decentralizing — you can build a trust node through what you put into the world
- Trust built through: consistent value creation, authenticity, real connection, unscalable actions

### 5. Permissionless World
- Don't wait for permission from some invisible arbiter.
- Technology cracked the walls of credentialism.
- You don't need a stamp of approval. Just create things of value.
- "Good things come to those who tap themselves in."
- Stop waiting for permission to live the life you want.

- **Andy (AAC priority):** Start ugly. Doesn't matter that the first AAC setup won't be perfect. Start.
- **Trading:** Violent consistency over time builds skill. 1,000 disciplined trades.
- **Content/writing:** Just publish. Ugly first version beats perfect draft that never ships.
- **Building trust:** In relationships, business, and market reputation — slow, consistent, value-first.
- **Permission:** Don't wait for validation to pursue something that matters.
