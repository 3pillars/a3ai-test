# Gabor Maté — Emotional Truth-Telling Framework

**Saved:** 2026-05-25

## Core Philosophy
Emotional truthtelling = practice of self-liberation and body awareness, not forced behavioral change.

## 5 Practical Steps

### 1. Shift from Self-Judgment to Curiosity
- Stop blaming yourself for hiding feelings
- Ask: *"What am I afraid will happen if I show my true self here?"*
- Suppression = survival strategy learned in childhood, not personal failure
- Recognizing it as old "programming" removes shame → makes room for change

### 2. Identify Where You Are Failing to Say "No"
- Regular diagnostic: *"Where in my life am I not saying no?"*
- People-pleasing = major form of emotional suppression
- Watch for saying "yes" to keep others happy while your gut screams "no"

### 3. Allow Yourself to Feel "The Full Rage" Before Forgiving
- When hurt → do NOT rush to spiritual/intellectual forgiveness
- Drop into and experience raw anger, resentment, grief FIRST
- Forgiveness can become suppression tool if used to bypass messy emotions
- Paying full attention to anger lets it dissipate naturally

### 4. Listen to Your Body's Sensations
- Chronic tension, fatigue, digestive issues, flare-ups = "truth-teller"
- Treat physical signals as wake-up to boundary violation
- Mind-body unified: swallowing emotions = immune/nervous system absorbs impact
- Body says "no" through illness when you refuse to say it out loud

### 5. Differentiate Between Behaviors and Emotions
- Allow full expression of *emotion* while placing boundaries on *behavior*
- *"I am furious right now"* or crying = fine
- Throwing things or slamming doors = boundary on behavior
- Every emotion is evolutionarily wired, healthy, allowed to be felt
