# Weekly Research Findings — September 4, 2026

## 1. Quantitative Finance / Trading / Monte Carlo Simulation

**Key Insight:** Monte Carlo simulation remains the backbone of quant risk management and strategy backtesting. Key applications in 2026:

- **Risk Management:** VaR, CVaR, stress-testing portfolios under simulated market conditions
- **Option Pricing:** Critical for path-dependent/exotic derivatives; the Longstaff-Schwartz regression algorithm enables American option pricing
- **Backtesting:** Trade sequence permutation and bar randomization reveal strategy robustness — guards against overfitting by generating thousands of alternative "realities" rather than relying on a single backtest
- **Tech Stack:** C++ for production speed; Python (NumPy, pandas, Numba) for development
- **Quantum Computing:** Still experimental — no production advantage in trading as of Sept 2026
- **Emerging:** MLMC (Multilevel Monte Carlo) for derivative pricing efficiency; hybrid Monte Carlo + ML frameworks

**Actionable:** If running backtests, always apply Monte Carlo resampling. Single historical backtests are dangerously misleading.
