## 1. Quantitative Finance / Trading / Monte Carlo Simulation

### Key Insights

**Monte Carlo in 2026 - Still Fundamental:**
- Remains cornerstone for risk assessment, portfolio optimization, and derivatives pricing
- Primary use cases: VaR/CVaR calculations, stress-testing trading strategies, exotic option pricing
- Growing synergy with ML - AI discovers alpha patterns, Monte Carlo validates stability across synthetic scenarios

**AI + Monte Carlo Integration:**
- ML models inform trading strategy parameters based on historical patterns
- Monte Carlo then stress-tests these strategies against thousands of randomized market scenarios
- Dynamic ensemble ML + Monte Carlo for risk evaluation gaining traction

**Challenges:**
- Heavy reliance on prior distributions and assumptions (garbage in = garbage out)
- Critics note potential underestimation of "Black Swan" events
- Computational demands significant, but quantum computing offers future speedup path

**Key Sources:** Interactive Brokers, QuantInsti, Medium (Balaena Quant Insights)

## 2. AI Agents / LLMs

### Key Insights

**Agentic AI is the Big Shift (2026 = Turning Point):**
- Move from reactive LLMs → proactive, autonomous AI agents
- Agents now equipped with: environmental tools, memory/context, planning capabilities
- Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise apps will integrate task-specific AI agents by end of 2026

**Multi-Agent Systems Emerging:**
- Multiple specialized AI agents collaborating on complex problems
- Orchestrated systems coordinating across value chains
- "Think → Act → Observe → Repeat" self-correction loops

**Specialization Trend:**
- Rise of Small Language Models (SLMs) for efficiency + on-device processing
- Vertical LLMs tailored for specific domains
- Addresses compute demands, energy consumption, data privacy concerns

**Enterprise Integration:**
- AI transitioning from "experimental tool" to "operational partner"
- Embedded in operational layers - interacting with APIs, orchestrating workflows
- Autonomous research agents now conducting experiments with minimal human intervention

**Challenges:** Compute demands, ethical alignment, scalability, interpretability, security

**Key Sources:** Microsoft, IEEE, MachineLearningMastery, JHU
