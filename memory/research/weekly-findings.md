## 1. Quantitative Finance / Trading / Monte Carlo Simulation

**Key Theme: Quantum Computing Meets Monte Carlo**

- **Quantum Amplitude Estimation** is the big breakthrough — quadratically reduces samples needed vs classical Monte Carlo
- Practical applications: derivatives pricing, tail risk modeling, credit risk analysis
- Platform to watch: Q-CTRL's Fire Opal for running Monte Carlo on real quantum hardware

**Practical Trading Applications:**
- Still essential for stress-testing strategies, backtesting with randomized trade sequences
- Critical for portfolio planning beyond point forecasts — reveals tail risk, sequence-of-returns risk
- Common pitfall: using normal distribution instead of t-distributions or empirical data (fat tails matter)

**Variance Reduction Techniques:** Antithetic Variates still key for faster convergence with fewer simulations
