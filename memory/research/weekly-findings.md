## 1. Quantitative Finance / Trading / Monte Carlo Simulation

### Key Insights
- **Monte Carlo remains essential** for pricing exotic derivatives (Asian, barrier, lookback options) and multi-asset options where other methods fail due to dimensionality
- **Risk management applications**: VaR, CVaR, stress-testing portfolios, full distribution of returns under various scenarios
- **Strategy validation**: Stress-testing across thousands of randomized market paths to detect "lucky backtests" and assess real edge before risking capital
- **Forecasting beyond point estimates**: Generates probability distributions, confidence intervals, support/resistance zones for better entry/exit/stop placement
- **Fat tails matter**: Markets are non-linear; extreme events occur more frequently than Gaussian models predict
- **AI integration trend**: Hybrid frameworks combining Monte Carlo sampling with ML for more efficient stochastic analysis
- **Tools**: QuantConnect (open-source), Pineify, LuxAlgo's Predictive Monte Carlo Engine
- **Limitation**: Accuracy depends on input quality; can underestimate "black swan" events; computationally intensive
