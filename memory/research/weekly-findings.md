## 1. Quantitative Finance / Trading / Monte Carlo Simulation

**Key Insights:**
- Monte Carlo simulations remain fundamental for risk assessment, option pricing, and portfolio optimization
- New trend: Integration with ML (Random Forests, LSTM) for enhanced predictive accuracy
- Important for backtesting: helps identify "lucky backtests" and true strategy robustness
- **Actionable:** Use Monte Carlo to stress-test any trading strategy before deploying capital — simulate thousands of scenarios with randomized volatility and order flow

**Risk Note:** Historical data may not replicate future behavior. Markets are non-stationary.
