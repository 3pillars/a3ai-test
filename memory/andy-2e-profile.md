### 2. Math Environment

### 3. Neuropsychological Evaluation
- Find evaluator experienced in 2e autistic children (giftedness + autism)
- Ask: "How many twice-exceptional autistic children have you assessed?" → if < 20, keep looking
- Gives: cognitive profile, accommodations, IEP/504 documentation

### 4. Emotional / Identity Support
- Isolation risk is high — different from peers at every level
- Build identity: "mathematician who thinks in pictures" not "kid who can't talk"
- Therapist specializing in autistic giftedness (essential, not optional)
- Find parent community of other 2e autistic families

### 5. Cupertino Context
