## Resource Guide (Researched April 2026)

### TOP TIER — Most Relevant for Andy

#### 1. Davidson Institute / Davidson Academy ⭐ MOST RELEVANT
**davidsoninstitute.org | davidsonacademy.unr.edu | davidsononline.org**
- **Profoundly gifted students only** — typically requires 99th+ percentile on standardized tests
- **Davidson Academy Reno** — free public school in Nevada, groups by ability not age, highly individualized
- **Davidson Academy Online** — synchronous online school for students in US/Canada, live classes
- **Davidson Fellows** — recognition/thinking stipend for exceptional young people (Andy's math ability likely qualifies)
- **Critical caveat:** All programs assume verbal/synchronous participation. Andy cannot speak or write — this is the core barrier. AAC must be established first.
- **Action:** Apply when AAC is in place. Document Andy's problem-solving ability on video.

#### 2. Bridges Academy (Bridges Education Group) ⭐ 2e-SPECIALIZED
**bridgeseducationgroup.com**
- **Grades 4-12, college preparatory** — specifically designed for twice-exceptional students
- Campuses in Los Angeles + online options
- Published 2eNews.com and Variations2e magazine (leading 2e publications)
- **Bridges 2e Center** — research and professional development for educators working with 2e kids
- More accessible than Davidson for non-verbal students — understands autism+giftedness as co-occurring
- **Action:** Contact about online/remote options for Bay Area family

### GOOD SUPPORTS — For Family/Community

#### 3. SENG (Supporting Emotional Needs of the Gifted)
**sengifted.org**
- Nonprofit since 1981, focused on social-emotional needs of gifted + 2e
- Online support groups for parents, SENG Community Group Facilitator training
- SENGinars (webinars), regional conferences
- **Best for:** Parent community, finding other 2e families

#### 4. NAGC (National Association for Gifted Children)
**nagc.org**
- Largest umbrella organization for gifted education
- Standards, advocacy, local chapters
- Less 2e-specific, more general gifted education
- **Best for:** Understanding your rights, IEP/504 guidance, finding local gifted programs

#### 5. Hoagies' Gifted Education Page
**hoagiesgifted.org**
- Comprehensive resource directory — "all-things-gifted" hub
- Parent section, educator guides, conferences, online support
- Less specialized than others — good starting point for discovery
- **Best for:** Finding local resources, understanding the gifted landscape

#### 6. 2e News
**2enews.com**
- Publication by Bridges Education Group
- Articles on executive function, remote learning for 2e, summer activities
- **Best for:** Staying current on 2e research and strategies

### BAY AREA / CALIFORNIA SPECIFICS

#### 7. Gifted Homeschoolers Forum (GHF)
**giftedhomeschoolers.org**
- Formerly GHF, now Gifted Learners
- Strong California presence, homeschool-focused
- **Relevant if:** Cupertino district isn't meeting Andy's needs and homeschool is an option

#### 8. Summit Center
**summitcenter.org**
- Psychological services for gifted and 2e individuals
- Assessment, counseling, family therapy
- Bay Area / California based
- **Best for:** Neuropsychological evaluation, therapist referral

#### 9. Renzulli Center (UConn)
**gifted.uconn.edu**
- Research-based gifted education, teacher training
- Less practical for immediate family use — more educator/academic focus
