Dear Davidson Academy Admissions Team,

1. Whether your programs are accessible to students who are non-verbal and use AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) systems
2. The admissions process for a student in Andy's situation — particularly how abilities are evaluated when traditional verbal/written assessment is not possible
3. Whether online programs have any flexibility for students with autism spectrum needs

I would welcome any guidance you can offer, even if it is to tell us that Davidson may not be the right fit at this time.
