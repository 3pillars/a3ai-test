# Personal Philosophy Note — 金惟纯 frame (via Kelly Peng interview)

Distilled from 金惟纯 (《人生只有一件事》) interview Jacob shared Aug 27, 2026. Kept because it maps cleanly onto Jacob's existing 诸葛亮 trading discipline and the AI-era judgment thesis. Source video: youtube.com/watch?v=OeMuIUmT5FA

## The three ideas worth keeping

**1. 因 vs 缘 (cause vs conditions) — the load-bearing one.**
- 缘 = external, ever-changing, uncontrollable (market, tech, platforms, environment).
- 因 = your inner state, the anchor.
- Discipline = anchor on 因, respond to 缘 without being moved by it.
- This IS Jacob's trading seatbelt in Buddhist clothing: cash is a position, wait for YOUR signal, don't let the tape move you. Same as innerdev's "doing nothing is a position."

**2. 减法 not 加法 (subtraction not addition) as the AI-era edge.**
- Learning = addition (knowledge/skills) — machines now do this trivially.
- Cultivation = subtraction (clearing 污染/执念/包袱 so innate life-force shows).
- Sharper reframe than his: AI collapses the value of *accumulated knowledge* to ~zero, leaving judgment, presence, and *what you choose not to do* as the only scarce things. Same conclusion as Karpathy's "director not writer" — leverage moved from doing more to choosing well.

**3. 开窍 has two doors: extreme discomfort OR extreme comfort.**
- Hardship door: cornered → forced to look inward, swap thinking systems.
- Comfort door: got everything you aimed for → "is this it?" (marginal utility decay).
- The comfort door is the one that quietly catches successful people. Jacob is building toward exactly that outcome (automated income, family provided for) — name it before walking through it.

**Entry method:** 发生就是功课，结果就是成绩单，行有不得反求诸己 — turn inward on *your reactions*, root the practice in 老实 (honestly facing daily 柴米油盐), 用心安住 not letting head/emotions churn.

## Caveats I flagged (don't swallow whole)

- **反求诸己 is the most abusable idea here.** Turn it on your *reactions*, NEVER on facts you didn't cause. Andy's autism is structural (fine-motor barrier, AAC gap), not a karmic lesson to look inward about. Know which is which.
- **"Raise life-force and money follows naturally (水到渠成)" is survivorship bias in robes.** He made his fortune FIRST, then found peace. Sequence matters — take the inner work, drop the causality. Especially for a solo operator supporting six.
- **"Real cultivation = people want to be near you; fake = always proving you know more"** — good heuristic but can quietly punish disagreement/rigor as "ego." Jacob values pushback; that's truth-seeking, not spiritual immaturity.

## The one line to pin to the wall

**父母自己先活好，就是给孩子最大的礼物** — for a family of six with a 2e kid whose needs could consume every hour, this is a load-bearing operational rule, not soft comfort. A depleted parent optimizing entirely around the child is a worse outcome than a whole parent with boundaries. See [[andy-2e-profile]].
