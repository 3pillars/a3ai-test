# Jacob's Hard-Won Trading Psychology (Internalized 2026-09-16)

## Core Mental Models

### 1. Shift Bias Fast With New Information
- No ego about what you thought or said last week
- Being wrong and changing your mind = intelligence, not weakness
- The cost of being wrong is always higher than the cost of admitting you were wrong

### 2. Gut First, Brain Justifies
- Brain will find 100 reasons why the gut decision is wrong
- Brain is risk-averse by default — it's designed to keep you safe, not to make money
- Gut has pattern recognition from screen time — listen to it, but make brain document the thesis
- Rule: if gut says buy and brain finds 3+ reasons against, still buy but smaller size

### 3. Move On Immediately
- No dwelling on fumbles or missed opportunities
- Only the *next* decision matters
- Yesterday's P&L is irrelevant to today's setup
- Journal it, learn the lesson once, then let go

### 4. Reduce Assets Followed
- Less is more — decision fatigue is real and expensive
- Deep knowledge of fewer assets beats shallow knowledge of many
- Quality of attention matters more than quantity of coverage

### 5. High Conviction + Patience = +EV
- Not needing to make money at all times is a competitive edge
- Patience is a position — cash is a valid position
- The market rewards conviction, not cleverness

### 6. Don't Trade Away Cycle Winners
- Don't overtrade potential cycle winners for measly bear market profits
- Exception: random onchain stuff with very small % chance of sustaining (legitimate trade, not FOMO)
- Distinguish between "this could be a cycle winner" and "this is interesting alpha" — treat differently

### 7. HTF Range Returns on Alts = Major Red Flag
- Alt breaks out, then returns to the HTF range it broke out of = cut it
- This is almost always a distribution pattern, not a retest
- Only hold through this if supreme conviction exists
- Default: cut, move on

### 8. Be Aware of Pivot Points, Ignore Macro Elsewhere
- Know when you're at a high-pivot macro moment (FOMC, CPI, major geopolitical)
- Outside of those moments, don't act on macro noise
- Macro is entertainment, not signal
