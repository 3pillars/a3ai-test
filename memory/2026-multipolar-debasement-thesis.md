## Pushback (why NOT to go all-in on the consensus)
- **The narrative is now crowded.** NSS + Gladstein + Gromen + Dalio + Dixon/Balaji all sing the same song in one week. Per Housel: the most confident grand consensus at the highest stakes is exactly where to keep a skeptical hedge. Don't bet the decline on a timer.
- **"End of empire" ≠ "collapse."** Better analog = Britain 1945–70 (managed retrenchment, stayed sovereign/wealthy) than USSR 1991. US keeps reserve currency, energy independence, demographics, military #1.
- **Dalio talks his (long-China) book** + selective Big-Cycle determinism: he applies decline to the US, ascendancy to China, while admitting China's *own* "old economy on life support" — i.e., China is *also* late-cycle (debt, demographics, property). Honest read = **contested** multipolarity, not a clean Sinocentric tribute order.
- **RMB can't be true reserve currency under capital controls** — de-dollarization in *trade* ≠ reserve *replacement*. Gromen & Dalio both blur this.
- **"Hong Kong-style peaceful reunification"** is doing heavy lifting — HK's "reunification" ended in the 2020 crackdown; Taiwan has a military, democracy, and chip leverage HK never had. Coercion could escalate worse than Dalio soft-pedals.
