## Core thesis (money & assets)
- **Stop hoarding fiat for retirement** [03:13]. Musk: AGI ~2026, AI > all-human intelligence by 2030. Robots/AI push production efficiency up ~1000x → cost of necessities (healthcare, education, industrial goods) trends toward zero → "universal material abundance."
- **Industrial-goods DEFLATION vs scarce-asset INFLATION** [03:26]. During the transition, govts print money hard to pacify the displaced + prop up the old system. Result = split economy: electronics/cars/clothes get dirt cheap; **scarce assets AI can't replicate (prime real estate, energy) inflate massively.**
- **"Watts" = the future hard currency** [04:33]. Compute = productivity; electricity = food for compute. Musk flags China's enormous solar + power-output buildout. **Future money is fundamentally energy** → rotate from cash toward oil, mining, nuclear, grid infrastructure — energy ecosystems that own generation.

## White-collar work
- **White-collar jobs vanishing fast** [05:42]: AI can already do ~50% (accounting, law, coding, copywriting); only corporate inertia is the brake. Robot surgeons may beat humans in 3-4 yrs.
- **Experience + AI = superpower** [07:53]: 45+ shouldn't compete on stamina/memory. Human value shifts from "what you did" to "what you choose to do." Most valuable middle-age assets: **judgment, taste, aesthetics, deep understanding of human nature** — what the "strongest intern" (AI) can't generate.

## Education
- **Traditional university function is dead** [08:43]: within 5 yrs AI gives better/more patient/free personalized tutoring than Harvard profs. College only worth it for adult social experience.
- **Cultivate EQ + resilience (逆商)** [11:15]: in an IQ-surplus world, solving messy real problems + staying calm in conflict is scarcest. Let kids build/sweat/fail in the real world, not memorize stale facts.

## Psychological defense
- **Cherish real-world imperfection** [14:23]: when AI solves survival, humans risk a void. Handmade warmth + family friction become the luxury emotional anchors.

## Action playbook [12:02]
- **Financially = pessimistic defender**: see fiat debasement clearly; shift assets into energy, compute, and core scarce assets to fight the printing press.
- **Spiritually = optimistic creator**: invest energy in what machines can't take — health, family, personal creativity.
