# Andy — Coaching Plan (maximize potential)

**Created:** 2026-08-06
**Trigger:** Hong Wang Fields Medal article (Le Monde) — China "spots geniuses but doesn't nurture those still struggling." Andy is both the spotted genius AND the one struggling (communication/motor). The risk is the system filters him out by measuring the wrong thing (verbal/written output) instead of nurturing what he has (systemizing/reasoning).

## The core lesson from the article
Hong Wang's math score was **just below the cutoff** at Peking U. The system redirected her to earth sciences. She only made it because (a) she independently studied first-year math anyway, and (b) there was a **transfer pathway** once she could prove herself. Genius nearly lost to a filter that measured the wrong thing at the wrong moment.

**Andy's version of "the cutoff" is not math ability — it's the output channel.** He computes internally at Putnam/IMO level but cannot speak or write. Every standard "prove yourself" gate (exams, verbal class participation, written work) is a filter he fails for reasons that have nothing to do with his mind. So the entire coaching mission is: **remove the output bottleneck and build the transfer pathway BEFORE trying to accelerate the math.**

## Order of operations (leverage-ranked — do NOT skip ahead)
1. **Communication channel (AAC)** — everything else is blocked until this exists
2. **Documentation of ability** — video/artifact evidence, so no gatekeeper can dismiss him
3. **A mentor who thinks at his level** — the "transfer pathway" that Wang had
4. **Regulation/sensory foundation** — so he can actually access the work (running/OT)
5. **Acceleration & enrichment** — only meaningful once 1–4 exist

## Pillar 1 — Communication (the cutoff-breaker)
- Get an AAC eval from an SLP experienced with **non-speaking autistic + high cognition** (not generic AAC).
- Push for a **robust symbol/text system** (Proloquo2Go, TD Snap, or a spelling-to-communicate/RPM-adjacent method if he shows literacy). Do NOT start with a limited picture board — his vocabulary ceiling is very high; a toddler-level board will insult and bore him.
- Because he can *draw*: build a workflow where AAC + drawing = his "voice." Drawing is already his proven output — treat it as a first-class channel, not a workaround.
- **Weekly coaching rep:** 10–15 min/day of low-pressure AAC modeling on topics he *loves* (math, not "point to the apple"). Model, don't quiz.

## Pillar 2 — Build the evidence file (so no one can redirect him)
- Wang needed proof to transfer. Andy needs a **portfolio** because his ability is so implausible that any evaluator will default to disbelief.
- For each demonstrated problem (Windmill, 2025×2025 tiling→2112, stoichiometry, QM): **film it** — problem shown, Andy's response, timestamp. Keep a running log.
- This portfolio is the key that unlocks Davidson, Bridges, mentors, and any neuropsych eval. Start it now; it costs nothing.

## Pillar 3 — A mentor at his level (the transfer pathway)
- One adult or grad student (math/physics) who engages Andy **as a peer mathematician**, adapting to his channel. Even 2×/month changes his identity from "kid who can't talk" to "young mathematician."
- Sources: Stanford/Berkeley math grad students, AoPS community, a retired mathematician. Frame the ask around the problems he's solved (send a clip).

## Pillar 4 — Regulation foundation (so he can access work)
- Sensory diet BEFORE focused sessions: 15 min trampoline/heavy work → then math. (See profile.)
- SI-trained OT to design it. Movement is not the enemy of the math — it's the on-ramp.

## Pillar 5 — Math enrichment (only after 1–4)
- Feed the gift at his real level: AoPS (self-paced), competition archives, open-ended problems he can chew on internally.
- **Don't drill "show your work."** His work is internal; forcing written steps punishes the disability, not the math. Accept the answer + optional drawing.
- Watch for the boredom trap: an under-fed prodigy disengages. Difficulty is the reward.

## What coaching actually looks like week-to-week
- **Daily:** 15 min AAC modeling on loved topics; movement before any focused task.
- **2–3×/week:** one rich math problem, filmed, added to portfolio.
- **Weekly:** review — what did he engage with? what frustrated him? adjust.
- **Monthly:** mentor session (once secured); check one institutional pathway off the list.

## How we know it's working (metrics)
- AAC: novel utterances/month (not prompted) trending up
- Portfolio: # of documented solved problems trending up
- Engagement: sustained attention minutes on hard problems
- Identity: signs he sees himself as a thinker (initiates, requests harder problems)
- Regulation: fewer dysregulation episodes blocking sessions

## Environment design — the "liveability" layer (added 2026-08-06)
**Trigger:** BBC "Four rules behind Japan's most liveable cities." Core principle = **liveability = reducing the friction of ordinary life**, and **designing for the most vulnerable user benefits everyone** (the wheelchair ramp also serves the stroller, the suitcase, the elderly). This is the *spatial/environmental* companion to Pillar 4 (regulation). Two layers matter: **hardware** (physical setup) AND **software** (people's habits around him).

**Hardware (reduce friction so he can access the work):**
- Fixed, predictable routines & routes = Andy's "shotengai" — everyday needs reachable without a stressful transition each time.
- Minimize transition/sensory friction (his #1 dysregulation trigger per profile): pre-signal transitions, keep the workspace stable, don't reshuffle his environment for novelty's sake.
- Treat AAC/drawing/eye-gaze as **infrastructure**, not special accommodation — same as a step-free station. Build the ramp once; everyone in the family uses the smoother channel.

**Software (manners in shared space — "manners matter as much as the hardware"):**
- The whole system around Andy (family, teachers, therapists) must **default to waiting for him to express, not speaking for him, not rushing him.** This is Andy's version of platform-queue etiquette — the culture that makes the hardware actually work.
- The best AAC device is dead without this social layer. Protect it deliberately.

**Rule 3 caution ("don't replace what works reliably"):** once a routine/tool/rhythm is working for Andy, resist swapping it just because something newer exists. Stability is itself therapeutic for him.
