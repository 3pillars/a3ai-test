## Position Sizing Framework (@thedefiedge)

**Core: "One Trade, Three Entries"**

| Entry | Trigger | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Test | Start of trend | Small initial position |
| Core | Price above entry | Add to position, raise stop |
| Press | Confirmed uptrend | Final addition |

**The Math:**
- Max Risk: 2% of portfolio per trade
- Max Position = Max Risk ÷ Drawdown %
- Example: $1,000 risk ÷ 40% drawdown = $2,500 max position

**Key Rules:**
1. Never buy full position on day one
2. Stop loss rises as position proves itself
3. **"Press from strength, never from hope"**

**Psychological Warning Signs (Position Too Big):**
- Checking chart every 10 minutes
- Moving your stop
- Losing sleep

## Ugly Old Goat's Teaching

**"A big trader chooses to become a small trader after making it big."**

- After a big win, deliberately reduce position size
- Don't stay at max leverage/size just because you "know" the trade
- The goal is wealth preservation, not maximizing every trade
- Taking profits off the table is the trade
- "Big" doesn't mean always being all-in — it means knowing when to scale DOWN

**Application:** After a 2-3x run, reduce position to 50% or less. Treat the rest as "house money." The house money can take more risk; your original capital should be protected.
