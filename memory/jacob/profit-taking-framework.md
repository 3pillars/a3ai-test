## Core idea
Two profit-taking approaches. Nova uses the second one.

**1. Systematic quarterly profit-taking** (the simple one)
Take profits every quarter in a bull market regardless of where you think the cycle is. No market timing required. Simple, but gives up upside and can look early.

**2. Stables-only portfolio (Nova's actual method)**
On every big win, split the profit:
- Part → **new projects** for compounding (2x on $20k > 2x on $10k)
- Part → **stables portfolio**, which is **never touched**
  - Not meant to catch new dips
  - Not meant to be fully exposed again
  - Purpose: never be caught without guard, always come out on top

Nova's stables portfolio is over 6 figures ($100k+). Her framing: *"I worked too hard for that already."* She is "close to 100% convinced" we're still early — but securing her future comes first.

- **One Trade, Three Entries:** 2% max risk per trade; press from strength, never from hope. The stables bucket removes the pressure to re-enter at the top — the "hope" entry.

## New Year Review checklist (Jan 1)
1. Total portfolio split: stable / deployed / deployed-at-risk
2. Stables balance vs 6-figure goal
3. This year's realized gains: how much moved to stables vs new projects
4. **Any profits NOT banked to stables?** ← the leak to look for
5. New project allocations still funded?
6. Is the stables portfolio still untouched?
