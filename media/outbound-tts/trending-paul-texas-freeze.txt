What is overstated. The summary says Paul argues price controls always lead to shortages. His words were that you end up with shortages and that it will backfire. It is a standard economics argument that a price held below the market rate causes shortages, but whether a Texas freeze would do that is a forecast. The summary also says legal experts question whether a governor can do this. The Fox News article I found quotes one person, the head of a conservative energy policy group, not a lawyer. It says a legal challenge from power providers is likely. The Texas Disaster Act does let a governor suspend some regulations, and a court has not tested this use.
