So the headline claim is real and it is corroborated. I checked it against well over a dozen separate outlets, and the major ones all carried the same core facts. Times of India, N D T V Profit, Money Control, News eighteen, Times Now, and several others. This is not a single outlet rumor. But it matters what kind of thing it is. This is a preliminary draft framework, not a signed deal, and not even an agreement confirmed by either government.

Here is what the draft proposes, according to Al Arabiya. Number one, Iran's highly enriched uranium stockpile moves out, either transferred to an agreed third country, or reduced in its enrichment level. Number two, the Strait of Hormuz reopens to international maritime traffic within seven days of an initial understanding. Number three, China could act as guarantor for the implementation and sustainability of any agreement. Number four, direct United States Iran talks happen in Doha instead of Istanbul. Number five, Iran's nuclear and maritime steps get matched by United States easing of sanctions, blockades, and economic restrictions.

There are two mechanisms in the draft that matter, and that most write ups are skipping. There is an independent verification mechanism that assesses compliance before each phase of implementation, so that neither side makes substantial concessions without corresponding, independently verifiable commitments. And there is a dispute resolution mechanism involving mediators and guarantor parties, designed to handle delays or violations and prevent renewed military escalation.
