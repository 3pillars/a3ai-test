Why the caution. Three things. First, Grok Bot now has a Finance integration that can link bank, card and investment accounts. Reports say it is read only, through Plaid. A bot that audits your life would want exactly that kind of access. Second, a security firm, Profero, reported in early November that one command from an agent gives root access to the bot's virtual machine, and that all bots in one account share the same credentials and files. Runlayer, a security vendor, reproduced the attack. Profero said it had not heard back from the vendor after more than seventy days, per Runlayer. Third, instructions hidden in content a bot reads can hijack it. A template written by a stranger is also a set of instructions. That makes a shared bot a trust risk. The community template list even includes a tool called Bouncer that scans share links and does not install.
