What the video adds. It is a one minute interview clip addressed to Anderson. I could not confirm the outlet or the date. In it Sanders says the election was a crying out for change, in part disgust at Trump, and says that sixty percent of Americans are living paycheck to paycheck, that the United States has the highest rate of childhood poverty of any major country on earth, and that millions of seniors cannot retire with dignity.

The paycheck number depends on the definition. Online summaries of surveys range from about twenty-four to seventy-eight percent. Some give sixty-two percent for self-reported, and about one third by the Federal Reserve's measure of not being able to cover a four hundred dollar emergency. These come from aggregator pages that cite other surveys, so treat them as rough. Sixty percent is within the range, not a settled fact.

The childhood poverty claim is overstated. A ranking of the O E C D, which is the club of rich countries, put out by Visual Capitalist, lists Costa Rica as having the highest child poverty rate, not the United States. The United States is high among wealthy countries, but not the top. I did not pull the exact U S rank.
