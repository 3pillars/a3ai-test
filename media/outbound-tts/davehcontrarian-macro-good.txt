David Hunter, at Dave H contrarian on X. He is replying to a thread of bears, and says macro conditions are really quite good: rates down and headed lower, the dollar beginning to roll over, jobs good, productivity good and getting better, inflation likely ready to roll over, earnings strong, and that you are caught up in the negative tape. It is a short reply with under a thousand views when I checked.

The negative tape is real. On Thursday the S and P five hundred fell four point four percent to seven thousand three hundred fifty-four, its worst day since April twenty twenty-five. The Nasdaq fell five and a half percent and is now eleven point nine percent below its high, a correction. Nvidia fell nine percent and A M D seventeen percent. That is from the A P and Reuters. The S and P is still about six percent below its October record, so this is a sharp drop, not a bear market.

Now his claims, one by one.

Rates down: mostly true. The ten year Treasury yield fell to four point eight four percent from four point nine zero on Wednesday, and was above five point three percent last month. But part of that is money running to safety as stocks fall, and four point eight four is still high. Headed lower is a forecast.

Productivity good: true. The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported third quarter productivity up three point six percent, versus two point one in the second quarter, and unit labor costs down zero point seven percent.

Jobs good: mixed. Jobless claims fell this week. But Schwab says payroll growth has averaged only about forty-five thousand a month over the past year, and September was twenty-nine thousand. The October jobs report comes out Friday at eight thirty Eastern, with forecasts near sixty-five thousand and unemployment at four point two percent. That is light, not good, and the real test is tomorrow.

Inflation ready to roll over: not shown. September C P I was three point seven percent over twelve months, up zero point six percent on the month. Core was two point six percent. Oil has been swinging because of the Iran war, and the Bank of England raised rates today and warned about inflation. Productivity helps, but this is a forecast.

Earnings strong: mixed. AppLovin and Tapestry beat, but the Wall Street Journal says disappointing results from two chip designers helped trigger the rout.

Dollar rolling over: I could not confirm it. The last dollar index reading I found was one oh one point six two on Wednesday morning.

My read. He is right on productivity and partly right on yields. The rest is opinion or unproven, and tomorrow's jobs number could settle part of it. He is a commentator, and I did not check his track record.

For you: this is a macro opinion, not a trade signal. It does not change your tripwires. The things to watch are tomorrow's jobs report and whether the Nasdaq holds the correction.
