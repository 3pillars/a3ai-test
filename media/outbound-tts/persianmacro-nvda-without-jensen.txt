What checks out. Anthropic confirmed on August fifth that it is building an in-house team to design custom chips for Claude. Meta announced an expanded Broadcom partnership in April to co-develop its M T I A chips. OpenAI designed its own chip architecture with Broadcom as the silicon partner, with small deployment planned near the end of this year. Tesla and SpaceX, with xAI and Intel, are building the Terafab chip plant, with an initial sixteen point eight billion dollars. Nvidia paid Poolside about six billion dollars to license its model tooling and hired one hundred nine people for its open Nemotron models. Nvidia's Hugging Face deal is about twelve point nine billion dollars. Nvidia paid about twenty billion dollars for Groq assets late last year. Reuters reported on October twenty-eighth that the Justice Department is asking questions about the Hugging Face deal. Last quarter's gross margin was seventy-four point eight percent, per Nvidia's release, so Mo's seventy-five percent plus is a slight round-up.

Two corrections. Nvidia's Poolside deal is a license plus hires, not a purchase, and Hugging Face is still pending. And the Justice Department's questions began before Jensen resigned, so his exit did not create that risk.

What I could not verify. That Jensen argued custom chips are not more cost-efficient. That A I now automates porting code off Nvidia's software, which Mo says kills its moat. That recursive self-improvement will force every lab to build chips. And his aside about Elon's mood, which is a hint with no evidence.

My read. The trend is real: big customers are building chips. But the conclusion, that Nvidia likely fails without Jensen, is a forecast. Against it, Nvidia reported revenue of one hundred fifteen point eight billion dollars last quarter, up one hundred three percent from a year ago. This is a multi-year thesis, not a signal for this week.
