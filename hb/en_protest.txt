## A protest sparked by a deadly bus crash in a western Iranian city turned into a new anti-regime protest with hundreds chanting "Death to the dictator."

A fatal bus crash which killed 13 oil workers in the western Iranian city of Abdanan sent hundreds of people marching in protest first against what they called local authorities' state incompetence, but then their slogans switched against state officials and institutions of the Islamic Republic.

![Image 54](https://www.euronews.com/images/logos/logo-euronews-stacked-outlined-72x72-grey-9.svg)ADVERTISEMENT

[](https://www.optidigital.com/?utm_medium=aio&utm_source=euronews.com&utm_campaign=content-1)

![Image 55](https://www.euronews.com/images/logos/logo-euronews-stacked-outlined-72x72-grey-9.svg)ADVERTISEMENT

Rare videos which trickled out onto social media showed hundreds of protesters changing slogans such as "Death to the dictator", "Islamic Republic, where is your cardboard leader?," as a reference to Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei, "Death to the incompetent official" as well as pro-monarchy chants such as "Long live the Shah." However, as some of the videos may have had sound added or manipulated in editing, Euronews Persian cannot independently verify the authenticity of these slogans.

Other videos show slogans being chanted against Ruhollah Khomeini, the founder of the Islamic Republic, and banners of the Ministry of Intelligence and the Basij paramilitary group being torn up.

Euronews verifications show that the protesters were continuing on Tuesday evening.

Abdanan is a city in the south-east of Ilam province, with a population of about 24,000, while the total population of Abdanan county is about 48,000.

## Protesters call for no gatherings to be held in the name of bereaved families

Fars News Agency, closely linked to Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC), reported a gathering in Vali Asr Square in Abdanan, estimating the initial number of participants between 200 to 300 which then rose to about 1,000.

The news agency claimed that the protesters changed "Today is a day of mourning" and "Resignation, resignation", demanding action against those responsible for the incident.

According to Mehr News Agency, the families of the victims issued a statement calling for the circumstances of the accident to be clarified and those responsible to be identified. The agency claimed that the families asked for gatherings not be held in their name.

The Abdanan Security Council called on citizens to remain calm and to avoid responding to what it described as unlawful calls and actions that, in the council's words, disrupt public order.

## Abdanan, a flashpoint city during the nationwide January protests

Abdanan in Ilam province became one of the main flashpoints of the nationwide anti-regime January protests in which thousands were killed by security forces across the country.

[](https://www.optidigital.com/?utm_medium=aio&utm_source=euronews.com&utm_campaign=content_2)

16-year-old protester Alireza Sayyedi, who was shot dead on 9 January by the regime's security forces during the anti-regime protests in Tehran, was from Abdanan, drawing the nation's attention on the city.

His body was taken to his hometown for burial and his funeral on 11 January drew large crowds who chanted anti-government slogans.

The Kurdistan Human Rights Network reported that the regime military and security forces fired live ammunition at the participants during the funeral.

## "Alireza, may your soul be at peace"

Then in mid-February, large anti-regime protests and clashes with security forces erupted again in Abdanan, marking the 40th day of Alireza Sayyedi's death. Videos from the protest showed demonstrators chanting anti-regime slogans and honouring the killed protesters.

During Tuesday's demonstration, protesters chanted "Alireza, may your soul be at peace" to keep his memory alive.

The Ilam traffic police chief announced that the bus crash investigation blamed the driver, the company that rented the bus and negligence of the road maintenance office, while local prosecutors launched criminal proceedings.

[](https://www.optidigital.com/?utm_medium=aio&utm_source=euronews.com&utm_campaign=content_3)

The Ilam governorate declared one day of public mourning across the province and two days of public mourning in Abdanan county after this incident.
