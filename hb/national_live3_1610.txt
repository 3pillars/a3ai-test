## Crew unaccounted for after second strike on tanker near Oman, UKMTO says

A tanker sustained a second strike, causing a fire on board, while transiting the Strait of Hormuz about 13km north of Khasab, Oman, the UK Maritime Trade Operations said.

The crew is unaccounted for, while the extent of the damage remains unknown, UKMTO said in an updated advisory.

An earlier report said the vessel had been disabled by an attack that caused a fire in its engine room and the crew was initially reported as safe.

## Bahrain condemns continued Iranian attacks and reserves right to self-defence

Bahrain on Tuesday condemned continued Iranian attacks on its territory, saying its air defence systems intercepted and destroyed aerial threats before they could reach their targets.

In a statement, the Foreign Ministry described the attacks as a violation of Bahrain's sovereignty and territorial integrity and a dangerous escalation threatening regional security and stability.

"Bahrain reserves the right to take all necessary measures to protect its sovereignty and the security of its citizens and residents, including exercising its inherent right to individual and collective self-defence under Article 51 of the UN Charter," the statement said.

It added that Bahrain would coordinate with the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), under the bloc's Joint Defence Agreement, as well as with its allies and partners.

The ministry also called on the UN Security Council to take firm and deterrent measures to compel Iran to immediately and completely halt its attacks, violations of other states' sovereignty, interference in their internal affairs, threats to maritime and air navigation, and support for proxy groups in the region.

It further called for Iran to be held accountable and provide reparations, including compensation, in accordance with international law and relevant UN resolutions.

## Two vessels hit mines in Strait of Hormuz, says IRGC navy

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps navy has claimed today that two vessels, a supertanker carrying more than two million barrels of crude oil and a liquefied gas carrier, struck naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz.

In a statement carried by Iranian state media, the IRGC navy said the supertanker was hit in the western sector of the strait and the gas carrier was struck while navigating an unauthorised southern route.

It claimed the vessels had been receiving air cover from US fighter jets when they struck the mines, causing explosions, fires and bringing both ships to a halt.

The IRGC navy warned that vessels using the southern route faced an "iron and fiery wall" of naval mines.

## Iran planning dirty bomb attack in Washington, CNN reports

Iran is developing plans for a potential dirty bomb or chemical weapon attack in Washington targeting senior members of President Donald Trump's administration, CNN has reported.

The alleged plot includes specific locations and targets and prompted US officials to hold contingency planning meetings over the past week, the channel reported.

US officials believe Iran's incentive to consider an unconventional attack may have increased as it faces continued American military strikes and an economic blockade, CNN reported.

Intelligence had also picked up Iranian communications suggesting bomb-making materials may have been stockpiled inside the US, although officials disagree over the credibility of the information.

The intelligence pointed to Tehran developing options rather than preparing an imminent attack.

“Whether they actually have the personnel in place to execute is a matter of debate,” CNN quoted one official as saying.

The White House is monitoring potential threats but “at this time there are no credible threats to the homeland”, spokeswoman Lauren Bis said.

Iran's UN mission rejected allegations involving chemical weapons, saying Tehran “categorically rejects and unequivocally refutes” them. It did not directly address the reported dirty bomb planning.
