# One crew member missing after two missile strikes on LPG carrier in Strait of Hormuz

 Written by[Nick Blenkey](https://www.marinelog.com/author/nick-blenkey/)
*   [![Image 111: facebook](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/themes/marinelog/images/icon-facebook.png)](https://www.marinelog.com/news/one-crew-member-missing-after-two-missile-strikes-on-lpg-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz/#) 
*   [![Image 112: twitter](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/themes/marinelog/images/icon-twitter.png)](https://www.marinelog.com/news/one-crew-member-missing-after-two-missile-strikes-on-lpg-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz/#) 
*   [![Image 113: linkedin](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/themes/marinelog/images/icon-linkedin.png)](https://www.marinelog.com/news/one-crew-member-missing-after-two-missile-strikes-on-lpg-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz/#) 
*   [![Image 114: email](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/themes/marinelog/images/icon-email.png)](https://www.marinelog.com/news/one-crew-member-missing-after-two-missile-strikes-on-lpg-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz/#) 
*   [![Image 115: print](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/themes/marinelog/images/icon-print.png)](https://www.marinelog.com/news/one-crew-member-missing-after-two-missile-strikes-on-lpg-carrier-in-strait-of-hormuz/#) 

![Image 116: One crew member missing after two missile strikes on LPG carrier in Strait of Hormuz](https://www.marinelog.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/TaraGas.jpg)
Image: UKMTO

In a reminder that the Strait of Hormuz remains a dangerous place, [UKMTO](https://www.marinelog.com/?s=UKMTO)reported an incident seven miles north of Khasab, Oman, in which the crew of an LPG carrier abandoned ship after the vessel sustained two missile strikes. The first strike caused an engine room fire, the second another onboard fire

A [Reuters report](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/crew-abandon-gas-tanker-off-hormuz-after-being-hit-by-projectile-sources-say-2026-11-17/) identifies the LPG carrier as the Panama-flagged _Tara Gas_ (IMO 9290270) and says that one crew member remains missing.

The Indian Embassy in Muscat, Oman, says the _Tara Gas_ was carrying 25 crew members, including 24 Indian nationals and that 24 crew members have been evacuated ashore at Khasab. SAR operations continue for one missing Indian crew member.

The LPG tanker is reportedly owned by Global Gas Inc and managed by Sharjah-based [Matrix Maritime Solutions.](https://matrixmaritimesolutions.com/)

The [most recent JMIC overview of](https://www.ukmto.org/-/media/ukmto/products/update-114_jmic_advisory_15-nov.pdf?rev=be65cfbd755a41c4816feba6cef4401c) the Strait of Hormuz situation notes “IRGC attacks, attempted attacks, and/or harassment activity [has] persisted including UAV overflight,targeted surveillance of merchant shipping, and occasional VHF hailing. These actions continue to demonstrate Iran’s intent to assert presence along key transit lanes and maintain pressure on transiting vessels.

“Commercial traffic through the Strait of Hormuz remained at reduced levels compared to the 2025  
baseline. Independent tracking data indicated single to low double digit transits per day, while US  
NCAGS data indicated approx. an average of approx. 28 U.S. assisted transits per day over the last  
72 hours.“
