A tanker sailing through the troubled [Strait of Hormuz](https://metro.co.uk/tag/strait-of-hormuz/) was engulfed in flames after an attack, leaving a crew member missing.

The vessel was travelling through the passage off the [Oman](https://metro.co.uk/tag/oman/) coast when it is thought to have been struck.

It suffered the first attack yesterday evening, which led to an engine room fire, the UK Maritime Trade Operations said.

It was then targeted again in a second attack, which has left some sailors missing.

The agency said today: ‘UKMTO has received reports from a maritime security agency that the vessel sustained a second strike, which caused a fire onboard.
