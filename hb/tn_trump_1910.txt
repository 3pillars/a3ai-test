# Trump to discuss Iran war with Xi and Putin as he begins Asia trip

## US President holds talks with Australian, Indian and Japanese leaders in Manila

The National

November 17, 2026

[Add on Google](https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=thenationalnews.com)

* * *

*   Donald Trump has begun an Asia trip with talks in the Philippines, alongside leaders of Australia, Japan and India.
*   Mr Trump plans three-way talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, China, to address the ongoing Iran war.
*   The US President warned of unprecedented consequences over leaked intelligence suggesting Iran could detonate a dirty bomb in Washington.
*   The Asia trip follows Republican midterm election losses, widely attributed to the Iran war and a resulting energy crisis.
*   Mr Trump said he would press the Chinese and Russian leaders to "get involved to get it ended," adding: "I think it's very close to getting it done."

US President [Donald Trump](https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/donald-trump/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/donald-trump/") said on Tuesday he would discuss the Iran war with [Xi Jinping](https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/xi-jinping/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/xi-jinping/") and [Vladimir Putin](https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/vladimir-putin/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/vladimir-putin/") in a highly anticipated meeting during his trip to Asia this week.

Mr Trump began his trip with talks in the Philippines on Tuesday. Speaking alongside the leaders of Australia, Japan and [India](https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/india/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/tags/india/"), he said the four countries were planning drills with maritime patrol aircraft.

Asked whether [the Iran war](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/17/live-iran-war-bahrain-alert/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/mena/2026/11/17/live-iran-war-bahrain-alert/") would feature in the Xi-Putin meeting, Mr Trump said he would press the two men to “get involved to get it ended”. He added: “I think it's very close to getting it done”.

Mr Trump was also asked about a US intelligence report leaked to CNN that claimed Iran could detonate a “dirty bomb” in Washington. “If there is, they will pay a price the likes of which no country has ever paid before,” Mr Trump warned. “I hope it's not, but we take it very seriously.”

The trip is Mr Trump's first abroad since a heavy defeat for his Republican party in [US midterm elections](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/us/2026/11/03/us-midterm-elections-trump-iran/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/us/2026/11/03/us-midterm-elections-trump-iran/"), a result that was widely blamed on the Iran war and resulting energy crunch. The US President has expressed interest in meeting North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, and said on Monday it might happen in China this week.

The meeting with Mr Putin also follows [talks in the UAE](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/17/president-sheikh-mohamed-thanked-for-hosting-crucial-peace-talks-in-meeting-with-russian-security-chief/ "https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/17/president-sheikh-mohamed-thanked-for-hosting-crucial-peace-talks-in-meeting-with-russian-security-chief/") involving Russian, Ukrainian and US negotiators last week. The peace talks were the first since February and resulted in an agreement on exchanging prisoners.
