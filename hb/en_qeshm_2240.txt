## Iran says the US military carried out several attacks on Qeshm Island in southern Iran, while at least five ships were set on fire by mines in the Strait of Hormuz earlier in the day.

Iranian officials said the US military conducted airstrikes on Qeshm Island in southern Iran on Tuesday evening, with locals reporting at least five explosions.

![Image 55](https://www.euronews.com/images/logos/logo-euronews-stacked-outlined-72x72-grey-9.svg)ADVERTISEMENT

Ahmad Nafisi, the deputy governor of Hormozgan, told the official IRNA news agency that the US strikes did not caused damage to residential or commercial districts, adding that "the situation on Qeshm Island is under control and all relief, service and executive bodies are on full alert."

Iranian news agency IRNA reported that "the sound of five to six explosions was heard in the city of Qeshm," while the semi-official Fars news agency wrote quoting "residents of Qeshm Island" that the sound of several explosions were heard north-west of the city of Qeshm. with smoke billowing at the same time.

US officials confirmed late Tuesday that US fighter jets struck Iranian missile launch sites on the island, in the same pattern as over the previous days, in retaliation for Iran’s targeting of US personnel and assets.

The US airstrikes follow multiple incidents in the Strait of Hormuz, in which at least five ships hit naval mines while sailing through what Iran calls "unauthorised" routes in the waterway.

“Safe passage requires coordination with the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran, transit through designated safe zones, and having navigation systems switched on,” the Iranian army announced in a statement.

## Iran claims downing of US military drone

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) claimed on Tuesday that it shot down a US MQ-1 Predator reconnaissance and combat drone in the southern waters of Iran, near Qeshm Island. According to the Guard, this would be the eighteenth drone of this type that its forces have destroyed.

The IRGC said in a statement it used a "new advanced air defence system" which allegedly tracked and destroyed this drone at 13:45 local time.

Earlier on Tuesday, the IRGC claimed said it carried out a drone attack against a US radar system at Bahrain’s Sheikh Isa Airbase. Bahrain, home to the US Navy's Fifth Fleet, and US' Central Command (CENTCOM) said that they intercepted the drones, without any damage.

The IRGC said the attack was carried out in response to "repeated violations of Iran’s airspace by US military drones".
