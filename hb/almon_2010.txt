# Iran steps up Hormuz, Bahrain attacks as US hits Qeshm again: What to know

## A top Iranian official warned that civilian infrastructure near US or Israeli bases would be considered "dual use."

Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf warned the United States and Israel on Tuesday that civilian infrastructure near their military bases could be considered legitimate targets, as the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed a new attack on US forces in [Bahrain](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/11/iran-attacks-us-military-radar-system-bahrain-despite-talks-mediators).

In response to a New York Times report published Monday suggesting that some of Iran's dams and water-transfer projects may have been built to support underground missile facilities, Ghalibaf wrote on X that the US was “prepping the next round on civilian infra,” shortening the word infrastructure.

"New rule: Any dam, port, or power plant near a US or Israeli base is dual-use. We’ll apply your logic consistently and even-handedly," Ghalibaf wrote.

Iranian media reported Tuesday that the US had carried out strikes on Qeshm Island in the Persian Gulf. The attacks caused no casualties or damage to critical infrastructure or public service facilities, according to a statement by Hormozgan Province Deputy Governor Ahmad Nafisi carried by the semiofficial Fars News Agency. The US launched strikes on the island earlier this month.
